## Probation Policy

**Policy No:** MSPL/25-26/HR/001 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective From:** June 1, 2025

### Purpose
This document provides complete information about the rules governing the probation period of an employee joining Meridian Softworks. It also defines the process of confirming employees on probation.

### Scope
This policy applies to all new employees joining and currently on probation with Meridian Softworks Private Limited, including its group company(ies), collectively called "the Company."

### Period of Probation
All full-time employees joining Meridian will be on probation for an initial period of **ninety (90) days** from the date of joining.

### Confirmation Process
During the probation period, an employee's performance will be evaluated periodically.
1. Employees on probation are governed by all Company policies.
2. **Ten (10) days** before completion of probation, the employee must undergo a performance evaluation with their reporting manager.
3. The reporting manager completes an e-evaluation form.

### Probation Period Extension
If the reporting manager's feedback is unsatisfactory, the probation period may be extended by a further **thirty (30) days**, or employment may be terminated without notice or compensation.

### Employment Confirmation
If feedback is satisfactory, employment is confirmed via a confirmation letter.

**Note:** An employee's service may be terminated immediately without notice if found guilty of insubordination, gross negligence, dishonesty, embezzlement, or breach of the Company's integrity policy.

### General Terms
- **Appraisals & Promotions:** Employees on probation are not eligible for bi-annual appraisals.
- **On Separation:** An employee separating during probation must serve a notice period of **thirty (30) days**.
- **Leaves:** Employees on probation, except during initial training, may avail leaves per the Leave Policy.

### Tailoring Option
Exceptions to this policy must be approved by the CHRO.

### FAQ — Probation Policy
**Q: Can I avail a compensatory off during probation?**
A: No, employees on probation are not eligible for compensatory off.

**Q: My probation completes on 31st October — will I be eligible for the next bi-annual review?**
A: Yes, provided you receive your confirmation letter on or before that bi-annual cycle's cutoff date.

**Q: I joined 5 days ago and want to resign — what is my notice period?**
A: Your notice period would be thirty (30) calendar days, effective from the day after your resignation date, as this falls within your probation period.

## Separation Policy

**Policy No:** MSPL/25-26/HR/002 | **Effective From:** February 1, 2025 | **Policy Owner:** Human Resources

### Objective and Scope
This policy keeps the separation process simple and informed, defining the rules governing an employee's separation from Meridian Softworks and its group companies.

### Modes of Separation

**(A) Voluntary Resignation and Its Acceptance**
- **Resignation and Date of Resignation (DOR):** The employee sends a resignation email to HR, copying their reporting manager, with reasons for resignation. The DOR is the date the mail is received.
- **Exit Interview:** HR conducts the exit interview within a week of receiving the resignation and issues an Acceptance of Resignation / last working day confirmation.
- **Early Relieving:** Management may relieve an employee earlier than their notice period at its sole discretion. Meridian is not bound to pay any employee relieved early who resigned voluntarily.

**(B) Involuntary Separation:** May occur due to poor performance, prolonged absenteeism, or disciplinary issues. Notice period or salary in lieu is at the Company's sole discretion.

**(C) Abscondment of Duty:** An employee absent for more than **five (5) working days** without informing their reporting manager is termed "absconded." HR issues an Abscondment from Duty (AFD) letter; if there is no response within 2–3 working days, termination and full-and-final settlement proceed, followed by recovery letters if dues remain outstanding.

**(D) Separation in Case of Retirement:** Retirement age is **sixty (60) years**. HR intimates the employee two months in advance.

**(E) Separation in Case of Death:** HR ensures settlement of accounts and return of Company property in coordination with legal heirs.

### Notice Period
As per the Appointment Letter; if not specified, **sixty (60) calendar days** (or **thirty (30) calendar days** during probation).

### Salary in Lieu of Notice Period
If the required notice is not served, the employee must pay back the shortage or a proportionate salary amount to the Company.

### Full and Final Settlement
The employee must clear all dues and submit a Full and Final Settlement Form on their last working day. Settlement is typically completed within **30 calendar days** of the last working day, after which the relieving and experience letters are released.

### Amount Recoverable as Part of Full and Final Settlement
Any Relocation or Sign-on Bonus is recovered if the employee separates voluntarily within **12 months** of joining. Recoverable amounts must be paid before the last working day.

### Important Pointers
Upon separation, an employee shall not, for a period of **one (1) year**, solicit or engage with any employee, partner, customer, or client of the Company they became acquainted with during employment. The Company reserves the right to pursue legal remedy for violations.

### FAQ — Separation Policy
**Q: Can I adjust my leave balance against my notice period?**
A: No, accumulated leave cannot be adjusted against the notice period.

**Q: My device lease is still active and I'm resigning — what happens?**
A: You must settle all outstanding lease dues per the Device Leasing Policy's separation guidelines before your F&F settlement is processed.

## Employee Device Leasing Policy

**Policy No:** MSPL/25-26/HR/003 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective From:** January 1, 2026

### Purpose
This policy enables employees to lease electronic devices (mobile phones & tablets) through Meridian as part of a structured compensation framework, enhancing benefits while enabling tax-efficient device acquisition.

### Scope
Applicable to all full-time confirmed employees who have completed at least **six (6) months** with Meridian.

### Eligibility Criteria
1. Employees must be confirmed (post-probation) full-time employees.
2. Only **one (1)** active device lease is permitted per employee at a time.
3. Not applicable to interns, contract employees, or employees on notice, PIP, or sabbatical leave.

### Policy Framework — Financing Options
1. Up to **90%** of the device cost is financed under the leasing arrangement, subject to the Leasing Agency's approval.
2. Standard lease tenure is fixed at **12 months**, with no deviation permitted.
3. Changes in lease terms or associated costs are borne entirely by the employee.

### Lease Process
Eligible employees submit a formal request to HR along with KYC documents; the request is processed subject to eligibility validation and Leasing Agency approval.

### Lease Deductions
Monthly rentals are deducted directly from payroll and remitted to the Lessor. Monthly lease rental must not exceed **30%** of the employee's gross monthly salary.

### Residual Value & Lease Structure
Lease tenure is 12 months; residual value is **Rs 2**. Ownership remains with the Company during the lease; the employee buys back the device at Rs 2 at the end of the tenure.

### Insurance & Maintenance
Insurance costs are borne by the employee. The Company holds no liability for device upkeep post-issuance.

### Theft & Loss Management
In case of theft or loss, the employee must immediately inform HR in writing, file an FIR, and submit documentation to support insurance claims. Financial implications from delayed reporting are borne by the employee.

### Separation Guidelines
If an employee resigns during an active lease, the full outstanding amount must be settled within **15 days** of submitting resignation. Non-payment may be recovered through salary or F&F settlement.

### Foreclosure Terms
Early closure requires payment of **8%** of the outstanding principal (including residual value), plus all pending rentals and any leasing agency pre-closure fee.

### Indemnity
The employee indemnifies the Company against losses arising from misuse, unauthorized use, or unlawful use of the device, surviving termination of employment.

### FAQ — Device Leasing Policy
**Q: Can I transfer a device lease from my previous employer?**
A: No, device leases are not transferable between organizations.

**Q: I resigned and my device was stolen — what do I do?**
A: Inform HR immediately and file an FIR on your own. Your F&F settlement will only process after lease and insurance formalities are closed.

## Learn & Earn Program

**Policy No:** MSPL/25-26/HR/004 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective From:** April 1, 2025

### Introduction
Meridian believes in continuous development of employee skills. The Learn & Earn (L&E) program monitors the performance of graduate trainee engineers and associate consultants during training or initial project allocation, rewarding successful individuals with a compensation revision.

### Purpose and Scope
Applicable to full-time employees of Meridian Softworks Private Limited and its group companies.

### Eligibility
1. Graduate trainee engineers elevated to Associate Consultant may participate upon successful completion of probation, within **12 months** of their initial joining date.
2. Interns or employees on notice/PIP are not eligible.

### Process of Evaluation
1. **Registration:** Eligible employees receive a communication at the start of each quarter to register.
2. **Discussion and Guidance:** Panelists (Managers, Technical Mentors, HR) monitor registered employees' performance during the quarter.
3. **Evaluation:** A technical assessment test and interview are conducted near the end of the quarter.
4. **Result & Reward:** Successful employees receive a compensation revision to their annual gross, effective the next consecutive month after the result is announced.

### Important Guidelines
1. Registration is mandatory to participate.
2. Employees may attempt the evaluation multiple times.
3. Compensation revision happens only once, after the first successful clearance.
4. Employees must not be on notice period until the revised payout is released, or the benefit is withdrawn.
5. L&E is independent of the annual performance appraisal process.
6. An employee who resigns within **six (6) months** of receiving the L&E payout must repay the additional gross amount.

### Important Timelines

| S.No. | Quarter | Registration Window |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jan – Mar | 1st – 10th Jan |
| 2 | Apr – Jun | 1st – 10th Apr |
| 3 | Jul – Sep | 1st – 10th Jul |
| 4 | Oct – Dec | 1st – 10th Oct |

### Important Technology Stacks

| Category | Tech Stacks |
|---|---|
| COTS Application | Salesforce, ServiceNow |
| Digital/Mobile & Web | React & Node.js, Angular & Java (Spring Boot), Flutter & Node.js |
| Data Visualization | Power BI, Tableau |
| Others | DevOps/SRE |

### FAQ — Learn & Earn
**Q: My Q2 L&E cycle wasn't conducted, and I cleared it in Q3 — when is my raise effective?**
A: The revised compensation is effective from the consecutive month following successful evaluation and result declaration, regardless of which quarter it occurred in.

## Leave Policy

**Policy No:** MSPL/25-26/HR/005 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective From:** January 1, 2025

### Purpose
Meridian believes all employees should maintain a healthy work-life balance. This policy defines leave entitlements and related rules.

### Scope
Applies to all employees (full-time, interns, trainees, employees on notice, probationers) of Meridian Softworks.

### Types & Number of Leaves
- **Privileged Leave (PL):** Confirmed employees are entitled to **eighteen (18)** PLs per financial year (4.5 per quarter), from April 1 to March 31.
- **Festival/Holidays:** The Company observes a maximum of **eight (8) days** of national and festive holidays per calendar year, in addition to PLs.
- **Maternity Leave:** As per the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961, and amendments thereto.
- **Paternity Leave:** Full-time confirmed employees may avail up to **five (5) days**, to be used within a week of the child's birth, for up to two children. Interns and employees on probation/notice are not eligible.

### Eligibility of Leaves
1. Full-time employees, including probationers, are eligible for 18 days of leave per year, accrued at **1.5 days/month**. A maximum of **4.5 days** may be taken per quarter without carry-forward.
2. Leave beyond 4.5 days in a quarter requires prior management approval to borrow from the next quarter; otherwise it is treated as Leave Without Pay (LWP).
3. Unplanned leave (same-day notification) is limited to **one (1)** instance per quarter.
4. Interns are eligible for **one (1) day** of leave per month, non-accumulating and non-encashable.
5. Employees serving notice period are not entitled to any leave.
6. Unauthorized absence for **two (2) weeks** is deemed abandonment of employment.

### Process of Applying Leaves
1. Advance email of at least **three (3) working days** to the reporting manager, with a copy to HR.
2. Leaves must be pre-approved by the reporting manager, except for illness.
3. Medical certificates are required for leave of **two (2) days or more** on medical grounds.
4. Leave of more than **three (3) days** requires approval at least **ten (10) days** in advance.

### Leave Without Pay (LWP)
Any unapproved absence, or leave exceeding the available balance, is marked as LWP and results in loss of pay.

### Leave Carry Forward / Encashment
A maximum of **eight (8)** accumulated leaves may be carried forward to the next financial year. No annual encashment is made outside of separation.

### On Separation
Confirmed employees are eligible for leave encashment at F&F settlement, calculated pro-rata. A maximum of **24 leave days** may be encashed at separation. Interns/employees under probation are not eligible for encashment.

### FAQ — Leave Policy
**Q: Can I borrow leave from next quarter for my wedding?**
A: Yes, with prior management pre-approval, you may borrow up to one quarter's worth of leave in advance.

## Dress Code Policy

**Policy No:** MSPL/25-26/HR/006 | **Effective From:** March 1, 2025 | **Policy Owner:** Human Resources

### Objective & Scope
This policy governs employee dress code and applies to all existing and new employees of Meridian Softworks and its group companies.

### Dress Code
The dress code for all employees is **business casual** — clean, pressed, properly fitted attire appropriate to the work environment, reflecting professionalism and mutual respect.

### Guidance on Unacceptable Attire
The following are not considered acceptable under this policy for all employees:
- Ripped, torn, or faded clothing
- Clothing designed specifically for sporting activities (shorts, sweatpants, tennis wear)
- Clothing more appropriate for evening or leisure wear
- Sandals, flip-flops, or open-toed footwear
- Caps or headbands
- Clothing with offensive words, terms, or inappropriate slogans

Managers are responsible for ensuring compliance within their teams; non-compliance may result in disciplinary action. Employees at client locations follow the client's dress code.

**Note:** It is mandatory for all employees to wear their Meridian ID card at all times.

### FAQ — Dress Code Policy
**Q: Is casual Friday dress different from the standard policy?**
A: No, the business casual standard applies every working day unless a specific team event states otherwise.

## Compensation & Benefits Policy

**Policy No:** MSPL/25-26/HR/007 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective From:** April 1, 2025

### Purpose and Scope
This document defines the compensation and benefits structure at Meridian, ensuring employees are rewarded fairly while complying with labor laws.

### Compensation Structure
Compensation is delivered through: (1) Annual Fixed Pay, (2) Variable Pay, (3) Long-Term Benefits.

### Fixed Pay
- **Basic:** Fixed salary before allowances, bonuses, or deductions.
- **HRA:** House Rent Allowance to cover rental expenses.
- **Special Allowance/Flexi Basket:** Additional flexible component of the salary package.

### Flexi Benefit Scheme
An optional benefit bucket including:
- **Leave Travel Allowance (LTA):** Tax-free allowance for travel within India, subject to Income Tax Act limits.
- **Meal Vouchers:** Company allows a meal card option with **Rs 6,600/month** available.
- **Communication Reimbursement:** Covers mobile and internet expenses.
- **Books & Periodicals:** Reimbursement for professional development materials.
- **Attire (Business Formals):** Reimbursement for mandatory professional clothing.
- **Driver's Salary:** For eligible employees using the car lease facility.
- **Fuel Reimbursement:** Monthly reimbursement for business-related fuel expenses.
- **Children Education Expenses:** School fee reimbursement of **Rs 2,500/month/child** (max 2 children); hostel fee **Rs 7,500/month/child** (max 2 children).

### Variable Pay
- **Performance Bonus:** Linked to individual performance rating, payable in January after annual review completed in December. Not eligible: employees on probation, notice, PIP, or involuntarily separated employees. Bonus quantum ranges from **40% to 100%**, pro-rated.
- **Sales Bonus/Incentives:** Based on individual performance targets, processed after financial year close.

### Long-Term Benefits
- **Milestone Income Plan (MIP):** Structured payouts triggered on completion of defined milestone periods.
- **Deferred Income Plan (DIP):** Similar to MIP, triggered after **eighteen (18) months**.
- **Phantom Shares:** Selected employees receive the monetary benefit of share price appreciation without actual share ownership.
- **Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP):** Kept outside the standard compensation structure; grant depends on grade, performance, and tenure.

**Important:** Employees serving notice or on PIP forfeit unvested/unexercised MIP, DIP, Phantom Share, and ESOP payouts.

### Non-Cash Benefits
Group Medical Insurance (GMI) covers self, spouse, and up to two children (up to age 25). Group Personal Accidental (GPA) insurance covers the employee.

### Payroll Practice
Salaries are disbursed on the last working day of each month, or the preceding working day if that date falls on a weekend/holiday.

### Compensation Review
- **Compensation Review:** Confirmed employees as on **30th June** are eligible for annual review; revision is effective **1st July**.
- **Bonus Review:** Confirmed employees as on **31st December** are eligible for bonus payout, processed in January.

### FAQ — Compensation & Benefits
**Q: Do phantom shares mean I own actual company stock?**
A: No, phantom shares are a notional allotment giving you the monetary benefit of share price appreciation — you do not receive or own actual shares.

## Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) at Workplace Policy

**Policy No:** MSPL/25-26/HR/008 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective From:** January 1, 2025

### Introduction
Meridian Softworks is committed to maintaining a workplace free of harassment, framed in accordance with the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013.

### Definitions
- **Sexual Harassment:** Includes unwelcome physical contact, demands for sexual favors, sexually colored remarks, showing pornography, and any other unwelcome physical, verbal, or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature.
- **Workplace:** Includes all Company premises, client sites, and any location visited during the course of employment.
- **Employee:** Anyone employed at the workplace regardless of employment type, including contractors, probationers, trainees, and interns.
- **Complainant / Respondent / Internal Committee:** As defined under the Act.

### Scope
Applies to all employees at Company or client workplaces, and to visitors, vendors, and contractual resources.

### Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)
Constituted at each location, comprising:
1. **Presiding Officer:** A senior woman employee.
2. **Member:** At least one senior employee committed to the cause of women or with relevant experience.
3. **Member:** Head or senior member of HR.
4. **External Member:** From an NGO or association familiar with sexual harassment issues.

At least half of nominated members must be women. Members hold office for up to **three years**.

### Complaint Mechanism
1. **Filing:** Complaints may be filed in writing to posh@meridiansoftworks.com or any ICC member within **three (3) months** of the incident, extendable by another 3 months for valid reasons.
2. **Receipt:** Complaints are listened to without pre-judgment; notes are kept strictly confidential.
3. **Conciliation:** If requested by the complainant, the committee may attempt conciliation before formal inquiry, generally resolved within **2 weeks**.
4. **Formal Inquiry:** The respondent receives the statement of allegations within **7 working days** and must respond within **10 working days**. The inquiry must conclude within **ninety (90) days** of the complaint.
5. **Interim Relief:** During inquiry, the committee may recommend transfer, additional leave (up to 3 months), or other appropriate relief.
6. **Inquiry Report:** Findings are submitted to the employer within **10 days** of inquiry completion.

### Action After Inquiry — Penalty Matrix

| Severity | Disciplinary Action |
|---|---|
| Minor | Warning, reprimand, written apology to complainant |
| Moderate | Withholding promotions/increments, rescinding bonus, transfer |
| Stringent | Compensation/salary deduction, suspension, termination, legal action under Criminal Code |

The Company acts on the Committee's recommendation within **sixty (60) days** of receiving the report.

### Malicious Allegations
Where an allegation is proven false or malicious, similar disciplinary action may be recommended against the complainant. Mere inability to substantiate a complaint does not, by itself, indicate malicious intent.

### Confidentiality
All complaint details, identities, and proceedings are kept confidential and not disclosed to the public, press, or media.

### Appeal
Any party may appeal to the appellate authority within **90 days** of the recommendations being communicated.

### Reporting
The Committee submits an annual report covering complaints received, disposed, pending, awareness workshops conducted, and actions taken.

A: Complaints must generally be filed in writing with the complainant's identity known to the Committee, though confidentiality is strictly maintained throughout the process and not disclosed publicly.
