# Harborline Customer Support Policy Handbook

Version 6.1. Effective for all support interactions handled by Harborline Foods Ltd.

Harborline is a subscription grocery and meal-box service operating in the UK, Ireland and the Netherlands. This handbook is the authority for every support decision. Agents cite section numbers when explaining an outcome to a customer. Where an agent believes the correct outcome is not covered here, section 16 governs.

---

## 1. Scope and authority

### 1.1 Who this applies to

This handbook applies to every support interaction across every channel: email, live chat, telephone, and social media direct messages. It applies equally to agents, team leads, and any automated assistant drafting a reply on the company's behalf.

### 1.2 Customer types

Harborline recognises three customer types, and several sections behave differently for each.

- **Household.** An individual consumer on a personal subscription. The default. Full consumer protections apply.
- **Business.** A registered company account, typically a cafe, small restaurant, or office. Consumer protections do not apply; the commercial terms in section 12 govern instead.
- **Partner.** A reseller or distribution partner operating under a separate signed agreement. Nothing in this handbook applies to partners. Route to the partner desk, section 15.4.

Where an account type is unclear, treat it as Household until confirmed. Applying commercial terms to a consumer is a compliance risk; the reverse is only a cost.

### 1.3 Precedence

Where two sections appear to conflict, the more specific section governs. Where any section conflicts with a statutory consumer right, the statutory right governs and the agent escalates under section 15.2 so the handbook can be corrected. Where a section conflicts with a documented account-level exception recorded on the customer record, the exception governs for that account only.

### 1.4 Tone

Replies are plain, direct, and free of blame. Agents do not speculate about causes they cannot verify. Agents do not apologise for outcomes that were correct; they explain them. Where Harborline is at fault, the agent says so once, clearly, and moves to the remedy.

Agents never tell a customer what another department "should" have done, and never characterise a colleague's decision as a mistake to a customer.

## 2. Service hours and response times

### 2.1 Support hours

Live chat and telephone support operate Monday to Friday, 07:00 to 20:00, and Saturday and Sunday, 08:00 to 17:00, local time in the customer's registered country. Email is monitored during the same hours.

Outside these hours, the automated assistant handles account queries, delivery tracking, and subscription changes. Anything involving a refund, a credit, or a complaint is queued for the next working period rather than answered automatically.

### 2.2 Response targets

- Live chat: first response within 2 minutes.
- Telephone: answered within 90 seconds.
- Email: substantive reply within one working day.
- Complaints under section 14: acknowledgement within one working day, resolution within five working days.

A "substantive reply" means an answer or a decision. An acknowledgement that the query has been received does not satisfy the target and agents should not send one in place of an answer.

### 2.3 Public holidays

Support operates reduced hours on public holidays in each country, published on the help centre. Delivery does not operate on public holidays, and any delivery scheduled for one is moved to the following day automatically, with notification sent 48 hours ahead.

Where a policy deadline falls on a public holiday, the deadline extends to the next working day. This applies to claim windows, cancellation notice periods, and payment due dates.

## 3. Accounts

### 3.1 Account creation and ownership

An account belongs to the named account holder. The account holder is the person whose name appears on the payment method at the time of registration.

Only the account holder may change the payment method, change the delivery address, cancel the subscription, or request account closure. Any of these requested by another person requires the account holder to confirm directly, through the account or from the registered email address.

### 3.2 Shared access

An account may nominate one additional contact who can manage deliveries, skip weeks, and update delivery instructions. The additional contact cannot change payment details, cancel, or close the account.

Two people may share one account. Two people may not merge two existing accounts; one must be closed and any remaining credit transferred under section 8.5.

### 3.3 Identity verification

Before disclosing any account information or making any change, the agent verifies identity. Verification requires the registered email address plus one of: the delivery postcode, the last four digits of the payment method, or the order number of a delivery in the last 60 days.

Verification is never waived, including where the caller is distressed, in a hurry, or claims to have verified previously in the same conversation on another channel. If verification fails, the agent explains what is needed and offers to send a secure link to the registered email.

### 3.4 Account closure

Account closure is permanent and irreversible. Order history, saved preferences, and any unused credit are lost. The agent must state this explicitly and obtain confirmation before proceeding.

Closure requests are actioned within one working day. Any active subscription is cancelled at the same time under section 6, and the notice period in 6.2 still applies, meaning a final delivery may still be dispatched and charged.

### 3.5 Data requests

Requests for a copy of personal data, correction of personal data, or erasure are never handled by support directly. They are routed to the privacy desk under section 15.3 the same working day. The statutory response period is one calendar month and the clock starts on receipt, not on routing, so delay in routing consumes the company's own response window.

## 4. Subscriptions

### 4.1 Plans

Harborline offers three subscription plans. Each is billed on a recurring weekly basis.

- **Essentials.** A fixed box, no substitutions, lowest price point.
- **Flex.** A configurable box with substitutions permitted up to the cutoff in 5.2.
- **Market.** Fully customisable, minimum order value applies under 7.3.

### 4.2 Changing plan

A customer may change plan at any time. The change takes effect from the next billing cycle, not the current one, because the current cycle's box has already been allocated in the fulfilment system.

There is no limit on plan changes and no fee. An agent should not discourage a customer from downgrading; retention pressure applied at the point of a downgrade request is a conduct issue, not a sales technique.

### 4.3 Skipping deliveries

Any subscription may skip up to 8 deliveries per rolling 12-month period at no cost. Skips must be requested before the cutoff in 5.2. A skipped week is not billed.

Beyond 8 skips, further skips are permitted but the agent should discuss whether a pause under 4.4 or a plan change under 4.2 better suits the customer's pattern. The 8-skip limit is not enforced punitively; no account is ever charged for a skip or refused one.

### 4.4 Pausing

A subscription may be paused for a minimum of two consecutive weeks and a maximum of twelve. During a pause the account is not billed and no deliveries are dispatched. The subscription resumes automatically on the stated date unless the customer extends the pause before it ends.

A pause beyond twelve weeks is not available. The correct action is cancellation under section 6, and the customer can resubscribe later. Any promotional pricing does not survive cancellation, and the agent must state this before the customer decides.
